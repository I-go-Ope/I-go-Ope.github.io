# Research

## Ultra-High-Energy Cosmic-Ray Composition

My current independent research uses CORSIKA air-shower simulations to investigate how cosmic-ray energy and primary nuclear species affect longitudinal shower development.

### Research questions

- How does \(X_{\max}\) change with primary energy and atomic species?
- How well can individual species be distinguished using shower-development observables?
- How do muon profiles compare with total charged-particle profiles?
- Can Cherenkov observables provide additional composition sensitivity?

### Methods

- CORSIKA 7.8050 simulations
- Python and Jupyter Notebook
- NumPy, SciPy, and Matplotlib
- Gaussian fitting and descriptive statistics
- Shapiro–Wilk normality testing
- Automated parsing of CORSIKA `.long` files
- Heatmaps and cross-species comparisons

### Current direction

I am expanding the dataset, improving automation and recovery of interrupted simulations, and investigating Cherenkov-light observables.

## Pacific Ocean Neutrino Experiment

I worked on the central timing system for the Pacific Ocean Neutrino Experiment under Dr. Robert Halliday.

My contributions included:

- Developing and testing a custom central timing board
- Calibrating synchronization offsets across timing channels
- Evaluating data-acquisition and signal-propagation pathways
- Supporting integration for the first P-ONE detector string
- Producing technical documentation for future researchers

This work contributed to timing performance substantially better than the original nanosecond-scale requirement.
