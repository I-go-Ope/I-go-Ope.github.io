# Research

My research interests center on **experimental particle physics**, **particle astrophysics**, and **detector development**. My current work combines large-scale Monte Carlo simulations, statistical data analysis, detector instrumentation, and scientific computing to investigate some of the highest-energy particles in the universe.

---

# Current Research

## Composition of Ultra-High-Energy Cosmic Rays

### Scientific Motivation

Ultra-high-energy cosmic rays (UHECRs) remain one of the major mysteries in modern particle astrophysics. While their energies exceed those produced by terrestrial particle accelerators, the composition and origin of these particles remain largely unknown.

My research investigates whether extensive air shower observables can be used to distinguish primary particle species and improve composition measurements.

---

### Research Questions

- How does \(X_{\max}\) vary as a function of energy and atomic number?
- Can different nuclei be distinguished using longitudinal shower development?
- How do charged-particle and muon profiles compare?
- Can Cherenkov observables improve species identification?

---

### Methods

This work utilizes

- CORSIKA 7.8050
- Python
- NumPy
- SciPy
- Matplotlib
- Gaussian fitting
- Shapiro-Wilk statistical analysis
- Automated CORSIKA `.long` file processing

Primary particles from hydrogen through iron were simulated over multiple energies to investigate trends in shower development.

# Pacific Ocean Neutrino Experiment (P-ONE)

From 2024–2025, I contributed to the development, testing, and deployment of the central timing platform for the Pacific Ocean Neutrino Experiment (P-ONE). My work focused on the design of a portable instrumentation platform, timing-system integration, detector testing, system validation, and technical documentation supporting the experiment's first detector deployment.

### My Contributions

- Designed and fabricated a portable instrumentation platform
- Integrated and tested the central timing system
- Improved cable organization and equipment accessibility
- Assisted with detector-system validation and deployment
- Contributed to documentation supporting detector operations

This work resulted in peer-reviewed journal publications, conference proceedings, invited presentations, and conference posters, all of which are available on the **Publications** page.
