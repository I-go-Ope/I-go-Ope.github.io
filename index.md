# About Me

I hold bachelor's degrees in Engineering Physics and Mathematics from Elmhurst University. My research experience includes the Pacific Ocean Neutrino Experiment, CORSIKA simulations of ultra-high-energy cosmic-ray showers, detector timing systems, scientific programming, and statistical data analysis.

I am particularly interested in using simulation and detector instrumentation to study ultra-high-energy cosmic rays, high-energy neutrinos, and extensive air showers.

My research experience includes work on the Pacific Ocean Neutrino Experiment (P-ONE), CORSIKA simulations of ultra-high-energy cosmic-ray showers, detector timing systems, scientific programming, and data analysis.

## Research Interests

- Ultra-high-energy cosmic rays and primary composition
- High-energy neutrino astronomy
- Extensive air-shower simulations
- Cherenkov and radio detection techniques
- Particle-detector instrumentation
- Scientific computing and statistical analysis

## Current Research

### Composition of Ultra-High-Energy Cosmic Rays

I use CORSIKA simulations to study how primary particle species and energy affect extensive air-shower development. My work focuses on quantities such as \(X_{\max}\), charged-particle profiles, and muon production.

[Read more about this project](research.html)

### Pacific Ocean Neutrino Experiment

I contributed to the development and testing of a central timing system for the P-ONE neutrino detector, including synchronization, signal-path testing, and detector-system integration.

[View my research experience](research.html)
