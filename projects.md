# Projects

## Coupled-Oscillations Educational Aid

Designed and constructed a physical coupled-oscillation system using carts, springs, a track, and a linear actuator. Developed Mathematica models connecting the theoretical and physical behavior of the system.

## Low-Cost Interferometer Kit

Designed and built accessible optical components for undergraduate interference experiments, with an emphasis on low-cost and 3D-printable construction.

## Portable P-ONE Timing Platform

Designed mounting, cable-management, storage, and transportation systems for the P-ONE timing apparatus.

## Cloud Chamber

Constructed a diffusion cloud chamber and used it to observe and distinguish charged-particle tracks.

## Small-Scale Rocket Project

Designed and constructed a water-propelled rocket with custom 3D-printed fins and a protected Bluetooth motion tracker.
